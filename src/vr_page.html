Revobots arm · VR teleop

connecting to the PC…
Nothing moves until you squeeze GRIP. Every squeeze is a new reference: the controller
pose then = where the arm is then; forward = where the controller points then.

Right controller (default):
  hold GRIP (side button) .... the arm follows the controller, release = it stops
  TRIGGER .................... close the gripper (as far as you pull)
  tilt / twist ............... tilt / roll the gripper
  A .......................... precision mode on/off
  thumbstick click ........... arm to the home pose (GRIP released)
Left controller:
  X = record start/stop   Y = redo   thumbstick click = status panel on/off

After Start you may put the headset down (front cameras facing your hands), as long as
it cannot fall asleep: proximity sensor off, or the sensor between the lenses covered.
